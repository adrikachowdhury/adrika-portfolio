GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

A little about my journey

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Asian University for Women, where I completed my Bachelor's degree in 2025, graduating Magna Cum Laude.

My academic interests lie in Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, and Generative AI. Through my undergraduate research, I explored Transformer-based models for analysing climate change discourse, gaining experience in text classification and model evaluation.

Alongside research, I enjoy building practical applications that explore how AI can be used to address real-world problems. My recent work includes developing a document-grounded research assistant using retrieval-augmented generation.

I'm continuously strengthening my technical skills and working towards a career in AI/ML engineering, particularly in NLP and Large Language Models.
