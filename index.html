About Me

A little about my journey

I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Asian University for Women in 2025. My interests lie particularly in Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, and Generative AI.

I enjoy exploring how language technologies work and developing intelligent systems that address meaningful real-world problems. Through academic research and hands-on projects, I have gained experience with machine learning algorithms and Transformer-based models.

I'm continuously learning and working towards becoming an AI/ML Engineer, with a particular interest in NLP and Large Language Models.
