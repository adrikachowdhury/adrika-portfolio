My Research

My research interests centre on Natural Language Processing, language classification, and the application of AI to real-world challenges.

Undergraduate Group Thesis · 2025

Exploring the Climate Change Dynamics through Multi-Aspect Classification

Tustee Mazumdar and Adrika Chowdhury

Supervisor: Dr. Abu Nowshed Chy; Co-supervisor: Dr. Tarem Ahmed

Department of Computer Science, Asian University for Women.


This research explores climate change discourse on social media through multiple text classification tasks, including relevance, stance, hate speech, direction, targets, and humour.

Using a dataset of 15,309 climate-related tweets, the study investigates Transformer-based models, including BERT, RoBERTa, DistilBERT, and ClimateBERT, alongside BiLSTM and MLP architectures.

DistilBERT achieved the strongest overall performance, with an average macro-F1 score of 77.8% across the classification tasks. The study also incorporated LIME for model interpretability.

Published Conference Paper · 2025

ClimateNet: A Multi-Faceted Classifier for Analyzing Climate Change Discourse

Mazumdar, T.*, Chowdhury, A.*, Chy, A. N., & Ahmed, T.

2025 IEEE 13th Region 10 Humanitarian Technology Conference (R10-HTC), pp. 105-110.

This paper presents ClimateNet, a multi-faceted classification approach for analysing climate change discourse on social media. It examines different dimensions of climate-related conversations to provide a more comprehensive understanding of public discourse.

*Co-first authors with equal contributions.

Manuscript in Preparation

ClimateNetBlend-X: An Ensemble Learning Approach for Multi-Aspect Climate Discourse Classification

Chowdhury, A.*, Mazumdar, T.*, & Chy, A. N.

This ongoing work explores an ensemble-based approach to multi-aspect climate discourse classification. It investigates combining predictions from multiple Transformer models through a meta-classification framework to improve classification performance across different tasks.
